Most products I get called into are stuck between two failure modes. Either the model is elegant but the app around it is a demo, or the app ships but the underlying logic is wrong in ways that only show up under load. Fixing either side in isolation rarely fixes the product.

What actually moves things forward is treating the whole stack as one thing. The Bentley MOSES automation, the FastAPI endpoint that serves the surrogate, the Flutter screen that lets an operator flip through fifteen thousand cases, and the DigitalOcean box that runs the nightly retrain. Each piece pulls the others into a specific shape. A one-person product practice can hold all of that in view.

That is the practice I run. Openct as Fractional CTO. The offshore digital twin, the patent similarity system, the ship recycling expert system, the SCCL mining computer vision stack. They are the same job in different clothes: pick a problem where the model, the data, and the product all have to agree, then ship the thing.
