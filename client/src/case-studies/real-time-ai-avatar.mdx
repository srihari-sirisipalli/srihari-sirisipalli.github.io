## The idea

Give the avatar your documents. Ask it anything about them. It answers, out loud, from a 3D face on the screen, entirely on the local machine. No cloud, no uploads, no bytes leaving the room.

## How it works

**You drop in documents.** PDFs, text, whatever the corpus is. They get chunked and embedded locally into a FAISS index.

**You talk to it.** Whisper transcribes speech in real time on the local machine.

**It retrieves.** RAG over the local index pulls the passages that answer your question.

**It replies.** A local LLM through Ollama generates the answer, grounded in the retrieved passages.

**It speaks.** Local text-to-speech turns the reply into audio. The 3D presenter lip-syncs to the audio in a browser scene.

## Why offline

The moment nothing leaves the machine, deployment gets simple. Defense, healthcare, legal, industrial R&D, sensitive customer data: the whole conversation about compliance shortens dramatically.

## Where it fits

Give it a training manual, it's an onboarding coach. Give it a legal corpus, it's a first-pass paralegal. Give it a technical archive, it's an always-available expert. Give it your own notes, it's a study buddy.
