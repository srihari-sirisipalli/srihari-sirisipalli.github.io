## What Openct is

Openct is a growing consumer platform. When I came on as fractional CTO, the product had traction but the technical foundation had grown organically. The mobile app was fragile, the backend was hard to extend, and the marketing surface hadn't been given the same care as the product itself. My mandate: rebuild it end-to-end without losing momentum.

## What I own

I'm embedded with the founding team, not an outside consultant. Day-to-day, I own the technical roadmap, architecture decisions, delivery cadence, engineering standards, and hiring input. I write code alongside the team. On any given week that means shipping features, reviewing PRs, unblocking blockers, and pushing the product forward from mobile to backend to website.

## The rebuild

**Website.** A complete redesign of openct.co: new information architecture, new design system, and content flow tuned for the lead-generation and product surfaces the business actually needs.

**Mobile.** Rebuilding the Openct Flutter mobile app end-to-end. New architecture, new state management, new design system, continuous delivery to production. Live on Play Store.

**Backend.** A fresh backend from scratch on DigitalOcean, with Redis for caching and PostgreSQL for persistence. Third-party integrations across payments, communication, and analytics. Async and background processing for anything that shouldn't block a request.

**Infrastructure.** Cloud provisioning, monitoring, and cost optimisation on DigitalOcean. CI/CD for mobile, backend, and website. Observability, incident response, and deployment automation from day one.

**Features.** Numerous new user-facing features shipped across web and mobile. Feature flags and phased rollouts so nothing goes out to everyone at once. Analytics instrumentation and iteration loops.

## What I care about

Product, not just code. The tech stack matters, but what matters more is that the team can move quickly and confidently. Every architecture decision earns its keep by making the next feature easier to ship, not by looking impressive on a diagram.

## Where to see it

The website and mobile app are the deliverables. Everything else supports those two surfaces.
