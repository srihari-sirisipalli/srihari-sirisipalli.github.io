## What it is

VHop is a mobile-first clubbing and nightlife platform for India. Currently in build.

## What is being built

**Club discovery.** Find venues by mood, music, crowd, and city. Filter by table availability and event calendar rather than static listings.

**Event booking.** Book tickets for events, guest-list slots, and public parties. Payment, confirmation, and QR-based check-in in one flow.

**Table reservations.** First-class table booking for the venues that support it. Minimum spends, package details, and party size handled up front so nothing surprises the guest at the door.

**Community squads.** Small groups of friends who plan together. Shared plans, quick voting on where to go, and coordinated arrivals.

**Paid host events.** Hosts can create and monetise their own events on top of the platform. Ticketing, promotion, and settlement handled in-app.

**Verified chat.** Communication between guests, hosts, and venues without exposing personal contact details. Reduces spam and lifts the trust floor for everyone involved.

**Platform token rewards.** A rewards economy that ties bookings, referrals, and repeat behaviour to a platform token. Users earn on activity and spend on perks.
