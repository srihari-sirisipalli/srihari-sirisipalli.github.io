## What this is

An autonomous underwater vehicle programme, on the product-development side. Vehicle design, subsystem integration, and team leadership across mechanical, structural, and systems work.

## What I own

Leading an engineering team through the vehicle lifecycle: concept, design, subsystem integration, and delivery. AUV work moves slowly compared to software because a bad decision doesn't crash a process — it can sink an asset. Rigour up front is not optional.

## The framing

Product development, kept high-level here. Specific vehicles, clients, and technical details stay with the parties that commissioned the work. What generalises is the discipline: how to run an underwater vehicle programme without the classic marine engineering surprises.

For the research side — structural reliability of underwater vehicle propulsion — see the [Underwater Vehicle Propulsion Reliability](/work/underwater-reliability) piece.
