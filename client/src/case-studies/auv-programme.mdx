## What this is

Ongoing research and product development on autonomous underwater vehicles: the vehicle itself, its subsystems, and the structural reliability of what runs underwater over time.

## What I work on

**Vehicle design and development.** Led an engineering team across a full AUV programme — mechanical, structural, and systems work.

**Propulsion structural reliability.** Assessing how underwater vehicle propulsion subsystems hold up over service life, and where the failure modes actually live.

**Marine, offshore, and subsea deliverables.** The AUV programme sits inside a broader body of underwater engineering work I've contributed to and led.

## The framing

This is research plus product development, not a shipped product with a website. It's the kind of work that produces vehicles, papers, and hard-won lessons about what fails, and why, when you put engineering into a fluid that does not care about warranties.

Descriptions here are deliberately kept high-level. Details of specific vehicles, clients, and results stay with the parties that commissioned the work.
